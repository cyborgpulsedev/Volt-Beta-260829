Volt PDF
GitHub

Read, annotate & interrogate
your PDFs — privately

A fast, ad-free PDF reader with AI built in. Renders 100% locally. Chat with your document using your own model — Ollama, LM Studio, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

Download Volt Windows 10+ · 120 MB installer · no admin needed
🔒

Fully local

pdf.js is vendored — renders offline, from file://, with zero network requests. Your documents never leave your machine unless you choose an endpoint that sends them.

🤖

Bring your own AI

Chat with the open document through Ollama, LM Studio, or any OpenAI-compatible API. One-click local setup with qwen3. Per-document model and persona overrides.

✏️

Annotate & mark up

Highlights, underlines, sticky notes, area highlights, rectangles, text editing. Export as Markdown, JSON backup, or annotated PDF. Bookmarks that travel with your work.

🔐

Sign & lock

Digital signatures verified through Apache PDFBox 3 + BouncyCastle. RFC 3161 timestamping keeps signatures valid past certificate expiry. Password-protected exports.

📄

Page management

Add, delete, reorder, insert from another PDF. Drag-and-drop with undo. Two-page book-spread mode with page-turn animation. Continuous, one-page, or spread view.

🔍

OCR & search

Built-in Tesseract.js OCR for scanned documents. 20+ languages on demand, cached offline. Search across embedded text and recognized text alike.

📤

Export anything

Annotated PDF, PDF/A-1b archival, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Markdown, TSV. Intelligent table and image detection. Everything built in-app — no server.

🔄

Self-updating

Differential auto-updates from GitHub Releases — downloads only changed bytes, not the whole app. Code-signed installer, no admin rights needed.

🎤

Voice

Read documents aloud with local voices. Talk to the AI with your microphone. External TTS/STT endpoints for higher quality. Voice chat in conversation mode.

Privacy is the product

Volt exists because most PDF readers are either ad-supported, cloud-dependent, or both. This one sends nothing anywhere by default.

  • No ads
  • No telemetry
  • No account required
  • No cloud storage
  • No upsells
  • Open source (AGPLv3)
